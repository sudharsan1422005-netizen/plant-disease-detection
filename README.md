# Plant Disease Detection

## Overview

**Plant Disease Detection** is an academic project focused on identifying plant diseases and providing relevant biological information about the disease-causing pathogens.

The project combines **plant disease information, bioinformatics, and computational analysis** to provide a structured approach for studying plant diseases.

## Objectives

* Identify common plant diseases.
* Provide information about disease-causing pathogens.
* Explore related biological and genetic information.
* Use bioinformatics resources for sequence-based analysis.
* Develop a simple and user-friendly plant disease information system.

## Project Workflow

```text
Plant Disease
      ↓
Disease Identification
      ↓
Pathogen Identification
      ↓
Gene / Protein Information
      ↓
NCBI Database
      ↓
Sequence Analysis
      ↓
Disease Information
```

## Bioinformatics Component

The project can use biological databases such as **NCBI** to retrieve information related to genes, proteins, and nucleotide or protein sequences associated with plant pathogens.

The retrieved sequence information can be used for further computational analysis.
